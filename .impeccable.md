## Design Context

### Users
Fleet managers, dealer managers, site managers, and operators in construction and mining rental operations. They work under time pressure, need live machine status, utilization, anomalies, and demand positioning without wading through decorative noise.

### Brand Personality
Trustworthy, practical, candid. Calm under pressure — a dependable operations instrument, not a sales cockpit. Precise about what is known; explicit about uncertainty.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm operational light theme (CAT amber / cream surfaces). Soft bento analytics layouts with chart-first metrics rather than identical stat-card grids. Avoid generic card walls, neon AI dashboards, glassmorphism for its own sake, and machinery-themed decoration that hurts readability.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with operational and cost outcomes.
2. Keep demand, supply, and utilization visibly distinct.
3. Show ranges, freshness, and confidence before asking for commitment.
4. Pair recommendations with lower-commitment alternatives.
5. Preserve human control; consequential actions stay auditable.
6. Prefer short, plain labels over role-heavy titles.
