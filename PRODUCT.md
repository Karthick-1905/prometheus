# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Construction and mining customer project managers and site engineers plan equipment
needs before work begins. Dealer managers and fleet managers balance availability
across regions without breaking existing customer commitments.

## Product Purpose

CAT Smart Rental Tracking helps customers obtain the right equipment at the right
time while avoiding idle rental cost and unnecessary commitments. Demand
forecasting provides four-week project and equipment forecasts, explains uncertainty,
compares flexible package options, and preserves human control. Dealer views turn
the same forecasts into guarded pre-positioning decisions.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, practical, candid. The product should feel like a dependable planning
instrument used in an operations meeting: calm under pressure, precise about what
is known, and explicit about what remains uncertain.

## Anti-references

- Revenue-first rental upsell experiences that always recommend more capacity.
- AI dashboards that hide uncertainty behind unsupported accuracy percentages.
- Decorative industrial interfaces that trade readability for machinery-themed
  effects.
- Generic card walls that obscure the planning decision and customer benefit.
- Autonomous recommendations that do not show alternatives or permit rejection.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the customer's operational and cost outcome.
2. Keep demand, supply, and utilization visibly distinct.
3. Show ranges, data freshness, and fallback methods before asking for commitment.
4. Pair every recommendation with a lower-commitment alternative.
5. Preserve human control and make consequential actions auditable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Information must not rely on color alone; charts require
accessible tabular equivalents; keyboard and visible-focus behavior are mandatory.
Support reduced motion, readable contrast, responsive field use, and plain-language
definitions for forecasting and machine-hour terminology.
